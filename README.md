HAVEN THE BOUNCER SLIME
------------------------------

This is a platformer game where you are a slime trying to get to the end of the obby. Throught the obby,
you will experience many diffrent emotions.

PS: If you get hit by the slug slimes 5 times, U DIEEEEEE....

u kill the slug slimes by hitting them 

Keys: A to move left, D to move right, spacebar to jump and left click to hit. U can also use the left and right arrows to
move left and right.


Have fun!
